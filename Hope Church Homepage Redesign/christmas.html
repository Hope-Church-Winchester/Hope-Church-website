Christmas at Hope Church Winchester: Good News of Great Joy

Christmas at Hope Church Winchester

Come and celebrate the good news of great joy

The first Christmas began with an angel bringing “good news of great joy for all people”. This is the birth of Jesus, God with us. Two thousand years on, that same joy is ours to celebrate.

Whether you're exploring faith for the first time, coming home for the season, or looking for a church family to belong to, there's a warm welcome for you at Hope this Christmas.

However you come, you're welcome

Come exactly as you are. Our services are relaxed and friendly, and you'll always find a hot drink and a friendly face. If it's your first time, look out for our welcome team, they'd love to say hello.
