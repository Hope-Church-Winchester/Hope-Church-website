Your first visit

What to expect

Coming for the first time can feel like a big step. Here's what a Sunday morning is really like — so you can walk in knowing what to expect.

Come as you are

There's no dress code and nothing to prepare. Wear what's comfortable — jeans and jumpers are just as welcome as anything else.

About 90 minutes

Our morning service runs around an hour and a half — a mix of sung worship, prayer, and a talk from the Bible, with coffee afterwards.

Easy to find & park

We meet at the Middle Brook Centre, just off the High Street. There's paid parking in the Middlebrook and Friarsgate car parks a moment's walk away.

Coffee from 9:30am

Doors open half an hour early for tea, coffee and a chance to say hello before things begin. You never have to sit alone.

Kids are welcome

There are age-appropriate groups for children during the service. Let a welcomer know you have little ones and they'll point you the right way.

You won't be singled out

You're free to simply come, watch and listen. No one will ask you to stand up, give money, or do anything you're not comfortable with.

Sundays at Hope
