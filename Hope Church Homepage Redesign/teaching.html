Teaching

Rooted in the
Word of God.

Every Sunday we open the Bible together and ask what it means for our lives. Catch up on any series, any time — watch or listen wherever you are.

Every week, we preach through books of the Bible — full series on Ephesians, Judges, Proverbs, 1 Kings, 1 Chronicles, Matthew and many more.

We're a church that believes the Word of God changes lives — so we teach it deeply, clearly and joyfully. New sermons are uploaded every week.

The Ancient Path for Our Modern World

Wisdom for living well in a complex world. We're journeying through the book of Proverbs — discovering how God's ancient wisdom speaks directly into the decisions, relationships and pressures of everyday modern life.
