Welcome

You are so welcome here

Join our student community at Hope Church as we grow in faith, do life and journey with Jesus together. Whether you're at Winchester University, Winchester School of Art or Sparsholt — or you're not studying but are student-aged — we'd love you to come along.

Fridays are filled with games, worship, teaching and much more. However you find us, you'll find a warm welcome.

We're with you

Becoming — or being — a student brings its joys, but also its challenges: the nerves of a new city, making new friends, and living out your faith away from home.

We'll dive into all of it together at Hope Students, and so much more. We want to journey with you — and remind you that Jesus has great plans for you.

Monthly socials

Once a month, it's a social! We've done all sorts — from Bake Off to Flip Out, games nights to Christmas parties. Come along and bring your friends; these evenings are always a blast.

Sunday lunches in the home

Grow in fellowship and chat over a home-cooked meal from the wonderful members of our church.
