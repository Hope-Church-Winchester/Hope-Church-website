Why we serve

We were made to help others

Jesus said he came "not to be served, but to serve" (Mark 10:45) — and as his followers, we get to do the same. Serving isn't just something the church needs doing; it's one of the ways we grow, build real friendships, and discover the joy of using what we've been given to bless others.

At Hope, we help people get to know and follow Jesus, and we equip one another to serve Winchester, the wider UK and God's global mission — bringing hope, joy and blessing wherever we go. None of that happens without ordinary people, transformed by an extraordinary God, giving their time and their gifts.

Whether you can give a few hours a month or a little each week, there's a place for you. Take a look below and register your interest — we'll follow up with a friendly conversation. No experience needed; just come as you are.
